## Slimy
---

### Basic Info:

* **Jam**: [Trijam-379](https://itch.io/jam/trijam-379)
* **Language**: Lua
* **Engine**: Löve2D
* **Time**: ~2.30H

### The Game:

Complete a tic-tac-toe while scaping from a (friendly) slime monster!

Click to fire, kill to place a piece into the map. Win the tic-tac-toe to win. Get killed by moster of touch it to lose!
